# KRL Teams — internal design research

Not shown in the public UI. Principles extracted from professional agritech operations products. Branding and layouts were not copied.

## Cropin Grow / Cropin Cloud
Useful: organisation → officer → farmer → plot hierarchy; one-touch overview; deviation/attention before vanity KPIs; plot dossier.
Adapt: Command answers what / where / with whom / at what stage / what needs attention.
Do not copy: product chrome, satellite plot dashboards, Cropin colour.

## OneSoil
Useful: map-first workspace; field list beside the map; select a place, then see its record.
Adapt: West Bengal as the primary information surface; district register beside the state.
Do not copy: NDVI rainbows, VRA tooling, or claim cadastral / satellite truth we do not have.

## John Deere Operations Center
Useful: Home + Map; colour fields by status; filters; detail for the selected unit.
Adapt: active / indicated / upcoming presence — not machine telemetry.
Do not copy: John Deere yellow-green, equipment panels, live machine dots.

## Climate FieldView
Useful: field as a dossier; imagery as context; scouting notes + photos as evidence; curated layers.
Adapt: Farm 360 as an operational dossier; media as a field journal, never empty “no media” tiles.
Do not copy: yield/moisture overlays or FieldView product structure.

## KRL rules taken from the above
1. Map-first geographic command, labelled Programme Geographic View.
2. Operation-first: happening / where / who / stage / attention.
3. Hierarchy-first: State → District → AC → Team → Agent → Farmer → Farm.
4. Evidence-first: progress links to media only when a record exists.
5. Density without card walls.
6. Team identity travels with every drill-down.
7. Imagery is content; if there is no image, omit the media object.
8. Knowledge-base-first: known → show; unknown → hide. No empty future slots, invented funnels, or fabricated alerts.
